  Hard-coded movement and gravity
  The advice to keep the physics simple and do the calculations yourself is adopted: no
  physics engine, hand-written formulas, and the numbers on the Build screen worked out
  by the same code.

  Scripted movement is not adopted. The student drives with throttle, brake and steering,
  and Mission 3 depends on when they brake, so the path cannot be fixed beforehand. The
  rover is simulated live, in fixed steps.

  One correction to the gravity point. Acceleration does depend on gravity once there is
  friction. Rolling friction and the grip limit both scale with weight, so both change with g:

     a = (min(F_d,max , μ_g × k_g × m × g) − μ_r × k_r × m × g) ÷ m
       = min(F_d,max ÷ m , μ_g × k_g × g) − μ_r × k_r × g


  Acceleration is independent of g only when there is no rolling friction and the driving
  force stays below the grip limit; with no grip at all, a wheeled rover could not move. A
  heavy rover on Mercury and on Pluto has the same mass but a different weight, so friction
  and grip differ, which makes it a good future experiment. Planets are future work (SRS
  Section 6), not part of the MVP.


  Mission 1 plan
  The prototype is milestones 1 to 8, played as a guest. Accounts (FR-41 to FR-44) follow it.
  Milestone 2 implements the Physics Model Specification.


     #          Milestone                             Done when

     1          Repository and checks                 A TypeScript and Vite project is on GitHub, and one
                                                      test runs on every push

     2          Physics core                          The Physics Model Specification is implemented
                                                      and tests T1 to T10 pass, including all 135 Simulation
                                                      sheet values to 0.001 s or 0.001 m. Starting build:
                                                      100 m in 8.500 s and 85 m in 8.150 s

     3          Build screen                          Part choice and the Part effects table come from
                                                      the physics module. The starting build shows 209
                                                      kg, 4.59 and 3.61 m/s² acceleration, and 7.37 and
                                                      6.08 m/s² braking on asphalt and sand
