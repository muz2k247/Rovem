| 2026-10-18 | **Levels B1: how the new physics rules are applied** (provisional; implements `docs/LEVELS_BUILD_PLAN.md` A6; judgment calls by Claude where A6 is silent, for k._muz to veto). (1) **Slope sign:** `step()` takes the slope *along the gear* (positive = uphill the way heading × direction points), not along the motion. While a rover rolls back, uphill-along-motion is downhill-along-gear, so the gear-based sign keeps one number meaning one thing; the pull along the gear is −m g sin θ. A6's "θ along the direction the rover moves" is the same number whenever the rover moves along its gear. (2) **At rest:** pushing forces N = drive + slope pull + outside force along the gear; the rover stays if |N| ≤ hold, moves along the gear if N > hold, rolls back if N < −hold. A6 says hold = F_r. **Judgment call:** while the brake is on, hold = F_r + F_b, so a held brake keeps a parked rover from rolling down a ramp (A6 says brake only opposes motion; this is the same opposition at zero speed). With the brake off it is exactly A6. (3) **`externalForceN` now acts along the gear and can start a parked rover** if it beats F_r (before: moving rovers only, and clamped at top speed). Magnets use it. A magnet or a slope can carry a rover past its top speed; the motor then gives no force (A6; DECISIONS 2026-10-14 above-top-speed rule). (4) **Rolling back:** `rollingBack` is true while the rover moves opposite to its gear; `speedMps` stays ≥ 0. A rolling rover has no top speed. W still drives along the gear and S still brakes. Resisting forces never reverse the motion: a step that would cross zero stops at 0 and the at-rest rule decides the next step. **Judgment call:** steering is mirrored while rolling back, as in reverse (A6 is silent). (5) **Water:** `upthrustN` lowers R (never below 0); drag F_w = b v uses the speed at the start of the step (one explicit step, like the rest of the model). **Judgment call:** a floating rover (R = 0) cannot change its heading in `step()` (A6: "no turning"); the drift layer also gives it a turning limit of 0. (6) **Helpers:** `ground.ts` (R, slope pull), `water.ts` (submersion fraction, upthrust, drag coefficient, floats), `magnet.ts` (force by distance; split along the gear and sideways, sideways positive to the rover's left). In `drift.ts`: `travelTurnLimitFromNormalRadPerS` (ω_max = μ_g k_g R ÷ (m v); 0 when R = 0) and `sidewaysBendRadPerS` (F⊥ ÷ (m v), ignored below 0.5 m/s). The old `travelTurnLimitRadPerS` stays for the Endless Canyon. (6b) **Gear flip:** `slopeRad` and `externalForceN` are given for the gear at the start of the step; when S alone flips the gear at rest, `step()` turns their signs itself (found by physics-checker). **For the game layer (B2):** `magnetOnRover` gives `alongGearN` and `sidewaysN` relative to the gear; while `rollingBack` the rover travels opposite to the gear, so flip the sign of the sideways bend when `motionSign` is −1. In a step that splits at top speed, `accelerationMps2` is the start-of-step value. (7) **Nothing changes by default:** with no slope, water or outside force the new `step()` returns the same numbers bit for bit (tested over 1500 mixed steps); T1–T10, the M4 step trace and the balance counts (20/9/23/17) are unchanged.
